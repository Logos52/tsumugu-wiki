Tsumugu Encoding Dictionary — a prototype of the Tsumugu project's dictionary layer. Entries are clean-authored from open sources and original analysis, using (but not limited to) the functional-component framework (form 形 / meaning 義 / sound 聲 / empty 空) and the Rules of Effective Memorization. Stories are labeled 故事 and optimize for memory; analysis is labeled 字源 and cites or abstains. Audio: Qwen3-TTS (Serena), batch-generated locally; browser voice as fallback where clips are pending.
