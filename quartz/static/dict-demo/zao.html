造
ㄗㄠˋ zào
verb — to make; to build · 做出來；蓋起來
造 = 辶 + 告

字源 FORM — what the parts really do

A foot on the road (辶) carries going; 告 gives the sound — together they wrote “to arrive; to go to.” Make and build ride the sound, not the picture.

辶
形a foot walking down a road — motion, goingused here for the original sense “to arrive”
告
聲gào — to tell, to announce; in other characters, usually the soundsame sound family as 浩、皓、誥 → see the 告 series

故事 STORY — a scene to remember it by

要「造」任何東西，第一步是走到那個地方。

To build anything, first get there: 辶 arrives, then the building begins. Feet first, walls after.

EVOLUTION

Drag on the waveform to select a slice, then 🔁 to drill it.

Early bronze forms write 造 as 告 plus a motion element (彳/辵), among other variants. The modern “make / build” is a sound borrowing.

告 itself: still “to tell, to announce” — the two characters share sound history, not meaning.
