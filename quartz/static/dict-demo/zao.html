造 = 辶 + 告

字源 FORM

A foot on the road (辶) carries the idea of going; 告 carries the sound. The graph originally wrote a word meaning “to arrive; to go to.” The everyday senses “make, build” don’t come from the picture at all — they ride the sound (see the meaning tree).ⓢ

故事 STORY

要「造」任何東西，第一步是走到那個地方。

To build anything, step one is to get there — the walking foot 辶 arrives first, then the building begins. (A memory hook, not history — the real link is sound, not logic.)

楷書 · modern

Early bronze forms combine 告 with a motion element (彳/辵). The modern core sense “make / build” is a sound borrowing — it never descended from the motion logic, which is why reading 辶 as “the road to creation” is a story, not an analysis.ⓢ

Compare 告: the donor keeps its own life as “to tell, to announce” — the two characters share sound history, not meaning.ⓐ
