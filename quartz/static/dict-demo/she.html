射
ㄕㄜˋ shè
verb — to shoot
射 = 身 + 寸

字源 FORM

The left side is not a body. In early forms the graph showed a drawn bow with an arrow on the string; over centuries of copying, the bow’s shape drifted until scribes regularized it into the look-alike 身 “body.” It does no semantic work here — it’s a fossil of a picture. The right side 寸 is a hand, releasing the string.ⓢ

故事 STORY

一個「身」體側過來，一「寸」一寸把弦拉滿 — 咻！

A body leans into the bow, drawing the string inch by inch — release! Wrong as history (the 字源 card just told you why), excellent as a hook. You get to choose which one does the remembering — that’s the whole design.

Oracle-bone forms draw the bow and arrow directly; bronze forms add the hand. By the seal script the bow’s silhouette had drifted close enough to 身 that later regularization locked the corruption in. This is the canonical example of why decomposition must stop at functional components: the modern shapes can lie.ⓢ
