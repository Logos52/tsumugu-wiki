The left side is not a body. Early forms drew a bow with an arrow on the string; centuries of copying wore the bow into the look-alike 身 “body.” It does no work here — the fossil of a picture. The right side 寸 is a hand, releasing the string.

一個「身」體側過來，一「寸」一寸把弦拉滿——咻！

A body leans into the bow. Inch by inch (寸), the string comes back — then the release: 咻, the arrow is gone.
