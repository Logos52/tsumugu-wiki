DEFINITION — two dictionaries, one entry

ENGLISH DEFAULT
lively · bustling · buzzing with activity
Of a place or occasion that is busy, crowded, and full of cheerful noise and energy.
簡明中文 · TOCFL B1-checked DEFAULT
（形容）人多、又吵又有活力，讓人覺得很開心的樣子。
像夜市、廟會、過年的街上那種氣氛。
