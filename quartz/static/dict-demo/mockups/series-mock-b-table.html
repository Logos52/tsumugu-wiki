例句 EXAMPLES

Hear 告 living inside common words you already half-know.

1
我想告訴你一件事。
I want to tell you something.
2
老師告訴我們，明天要考試。
The teacher told us we have a test tomorrow. 考試kǎoshì — exam, test
3
電視上的廣告太多了。
There are too many ads on TV. 廣告guǎnggào — advertisement
4
我下星期要交一份報告。
I have to hand in a report next week. 交jiāo — to hand in, submit報告bàogào — report
5
媽媽警告我，不要太晚回家。
Mom warned me not to come home too late. 警告jǐnggào — to warn
告訴 → 廣告 → 報告 → 警告: one donor, four everyday words.
The rhyme -ào survives in every member; only the initial drifts, and it drifts in bands. After two or three series, you start predicting characters you’ve never studied.
