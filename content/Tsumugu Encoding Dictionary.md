A dictionary built for **remembering**, not just looking up — the prototype of Tsumugu's dictionary layer, here for feedback.

Every entry pairs a **字源** card (grounded analysis: functional components — 形 form / 義 meaning / 聲 sound / 空 empty) with a **故事** card (whatever makes it stick, labeled honestly). Components are shown by color, not explained repeatedly. Everything readable is playable — Qwen3-TTS (Serena) where clips are baked, browser voice as fallback.
