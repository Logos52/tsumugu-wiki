17. **他打電話來的時候，我正好在看電視。** (Tā dǎ diàn huà lái de shí hòu, wǒ zhèng hǎo zài kàn diàn shì.) — I happened to be watching TV when he called. ![[audio/4ad32b62d3687363.mp3]]
18. **我想先看一下書，再去散步。** (Wǒ xiǎng xiān kàn yī xià shū, zài qù sàn bù.) — I wanted to read a book before going for a walk. ![[audio/56bbc87f19ca5dcc.mp3]]
19. **他習慣先聽一下音樂，再開始工作。** (Tā xí guàn xiān tīng yī xià yīn yuè, zài kāi shǐ gōng zuò.) — He used to listen to music before starting his work. ![[audio/dab4b7019574b930.mp3]]
20. **在離學校不遠的地方有一個銀行。** (Zài lí xué xiào bù yuǎn de dì fāng yǒu yī gè yín háng.) — There is a bank not far from the school. ![[audio/ef05c1141176e650.mp3]]
21. **醫生說生病的人需要多喝水，多休息。** (Yī shēng shuō shēng bìng de rén xū yào duō hē shuǐ, duō xiū xī.) — The doctor said that sick people need to drink a lot of water and rest a lot. ![[audio/a824bf64da993032.mp3]]
22. **說實話，我已經好久沒去電影院看電影了。** (Shuō shí huà, wǒ yǐ jīng hǎo jiǔ méi qù diàn yǐng yuàn kàn diàn yǐng le.) — To be honest, I haven't gone to the cinema to watch a movie for a long time. ![[audio/cdc8cceb9d9a8867.mp3]]
23. **說實話，我不太記得他的名字。** (Shuō shí huà, wǒ bù tài jì de tā de míng zì.) — To tell the truth, I can't quite remember his name. ![[audio/311c78388188811b.mp3]]
24. **同學們可以互相學習。** (Tóng xué men kě yǐ hù xiāng xué xí.) — Students can learn from each other. ![[audio/f621d09aa29caf7c.mp3]]
25. **老師讓大家在教室休息一下。** (Lǎo shī ràng dà jiā zài jiào shì xiū xī yī xià.) — The teacher told everyone to take a break in the classroom. ![[audio/570a4c30cddf44ee.mp3]]
26. **教授在上課的時候讓大家看了一幅非常有名的畫。** (Jiào shòu zài shàng kè de shí hòu ràng dà jiā kàn le yī fú fēi cháng yǒu míng de huà.) — The professor showed us a very famous painting during the lesson. ![[audio/34678cea8f64fc02.mp3]]
27. **老師讓我帶書回家。** (Lǎo shī ràng wǒ dài shū huí jiā.) — The teacher told me to take my books home. ![[audio/130d91c9abf8f3f6.mp3]]
