# 深入詞彙 — 這個專案要學的詞

> Deep dive into the 22 target words from this episode: reading, TOCFL level, meaning, character breakdown, a memory hook, and a simple example sentence. Readings + levels are from CC-CEDICT/TOCFL. Companion to the [[why-friendship-differs-summary|摘要]] (12 of these appear there).

## 封閉 — ㄈㄥ ㄅㄧˋ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** to close; to seal off; to close down (an illegal venue)

**拆解 / breakdown:** 封 (fēng) originally means 'to seal' — think of sealing a letter or a boundary; it carries the sense of shutting something so nothing crosses it. 閉 (bì) means 'to shut / close' and the learner already knows the related single character 關 (to close) and 門 (door); 閉 even contains 門 (door radical) on the outside, so visually it is 'a door shut from within'. Together 封閉 = 'seal + shut' = to seal off completely, or (as an adjective) closed-off and cut off from the outside. Stronger and more total than the everyday 關.

**記憶 / memory hook:** Picture a door (門, which you can see inside 閉) that has been not just closed but SEALED with tape (封). 關 = you shut the shop for the night; 封閉 = the police seal it so nobody gets in or out. Also useful as an adjective for a person: 封閉的人 = someone shut off, who lets nobody into their heart.

**例句 / example:** 他的心很封閉，不讓朋友看到真正的自己。
## 隱私 — ㄧㄣˇ ㄙ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** secrets; private business; privacy

**拆解 / breakdown:** 隱 (yǐn) means 'hidden / concealed' (the same 隱 that appears in 隱藏 below). 私 (sī) means 'private / personal / one's own' — the opposite of 公 (public); the learner may recognise it from 私人 (personal). So 隱私 = 'hidden + private' = the private matters one keeps hidden = privacy. A noun, not a verb.

**記憶 / memory hook:** Two 'keep-it-to-yourself' characters stacked: 隱 (hide) + 私 (private). If something is both hidden AND private, it is your 隱私. Tip: 隱私 is the THING (privacy / private business); the action 'to hide' is 隱藏. Don't mix them up.

**例句 / example:** 好朋友也要給對方一點隱私，不要什麼事情都問。
## 隱藏 — ㄧㄣˇ ㄘㄤˊ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** to hide; to conceal; to mask

**拆解 / breakdown:** 隱 (yǐn) = 'hidden / concealed' (same first character as 隱私). 藏 (cáng) = 'to store away / hide' — the everyday word for tucking something out of sight. Both characters already mean 'hide', so 隱藏 doubles up for emphasis: to hide / conceal thoroughly. Can take an object (隱藏感覺 = hide one's feelings) or be reflexive (隱藏自己).

**記憶 / memory hook:** A 'double-hide' word: 隱 + 藏 both mean conceal, so 隱藏 is hiding done well — nobody can find it. Contrast: 隱私 (noun, the private thing) vs 隱藏 (verb, the act of hiding it). You 隱藏 your 隱私.

**例句 / example:** 她常常隱藏自己的心情，讓大家以為她很開心。
## 營造 — ㄧㄥˊ ㄗㄠˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** to build (housing); to construct; to make

**拆解 / breakdown:** 營 (yíng) means 'to manage / operate / run' (as in running a business or a camp). 造 (zào) means 'to make / create / build' — the learner may know it from 製造 (manufacture). Together 營造 literally = 'manage + make' = to build / construct (originally housing). But the high-frequency modern use is figurative: to create an atmosphere or feeling on purpose — 營造氣氛 (create a mood), 營造形象 (build an image). Note the learner already knows 氣氛 (atmosphere).

**記憶 / memory hook:** Don't just translate it as plain 'build' — its most common real use is 營造氣氛 / 營造形象: deliberately ENGINEERING a vibe or an image. Think of a film crew 'constructing' a romantic atmosphere on set. Pairs perfectly with the face/image theme: people 營造 a successful image even when life is hard.

**例句 / example:** 他花很多錢，只是想營造一個有錢人的樣子。
## 炫耀 — ㄒㄩㄢˋ ㄧㄠˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** dazzling; to show off; to flaunt

**拆解 / breakdown:** 炫 (xuàn) has the 火 (fire) radical on the left and means 'to shine dazzlingly / blinding bright'. 耀 (yào) also means 'to shine / dazzle / glory' (related to 光耀, glory). Two 'shining' characters together = to make yourself shine in others' eyes on purpose = to show off / flaunt. The fire radical 火 is a good visual hook: showing off is 'turning on the bright lights' so everyone looks at you.

**記憶 / memory hook:** See the 火 (fire) inside 炫 — a show-off is lighting fireworks so all eyes are on them. 炫耀 carries a slightly negative, look-at-me tone. Strongly tied to the 'face' (面子) theme: people 炫耀 money, clothes, or trips to win face.

**例句 / example:** 他常常在朋友面前炫耀自己有很多錢。
## 掩飾 — ㄧㄢˇ ㄕˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** to cover up; to conceal; to mask

**拆解 / breakdown:** 掩 (yǎn) has the 扌 (hand) radical and means 'to cover with the hand / cover up' — picture a hand placed over something. 飾 (shì) means 'to decorate / adorn' (as in 裝飾, decoration). Together 掩飾 = 'cover + decorate' = to cover something up by dressing it over with a nicer surface = to gloss over / mask / disguise. Usually applied to feelings, mistakes, or the truth — not physical objects.

**記憶 / memory hook:** Hand (扌 in 掩) covering something, then 飾 'dressing it up' nicely on top — that's 掩飾: covering up the ugly truth with a pretty cover. Compare: 隱藏 = simply hide it away; 掩飾 = hide it by putting a fake nice face over it. Perfect for the 'saving face' theme.

**例句 / example:** 他想掩飾自己沒有錢，所以一直假裝很開心。
## 防備 — ㄈㄤˊ ㄅㄟˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** to guard against

**拆解 / breakdown:** A two-character compound of two near-synonyms. 防 (ㄈㄤˊ) means to defend / guard against / prevent — you may know it from 預防 (prevent) or 防火 (fireproof); its 阜 (mound/wall) radical on the left hints at a protective barrier. 備 (ㄅㄟˋ) means to prepare / be ready / equip — the same 備 in 準備 (to prepare), which you already know. So 防+備 literally stacks 'guard against' onto 'be prepared' = to be on guard, to take precautions against something.

**記憶 / memory hook:** Think 準備 (prepare) but with a defensive twist: you are not just preparing for a party, you are preparing AGAINST a threat. If you already know 準備, just swap 準 for 防 (defend) and you get 'prepare-to-defend' = 防備. The wall radical 阜 in 防 is your mental fortress.

**例句 / example:** 在陌生的地方，我們對人有一點防備。
## 飢渴 — ㄐㄧ ㄎㄜˇ · TOCFL-7
**意思 / meaning:** hungry and thirsty; (fig.) to crave (knowledge, love etc)

**拆解 / breakdown:** Two characters that both carry a 'body lacking something' feeling. 飢 (ㄐㄧ) = hungry / famished; its left side is 食 (food, shrunk to the 飠 radical), so it literally pictures wanting food. 渴 (ㄎㄜˇ) = thirsty; its left side is the three-dot water radical 氵, picturing wanting water. Put 飢 (hunger for food) + 渴 (thirst for water) together and you get the whole-body state of being both hungry and thirsty — and by extension a burning craving for non-physical things like 知識 (knowledge) or 愛 (love).

**記憶 / memory hook:** Read the two radicals like a comic strip: 飠 (food) + 氵 (water) = your stomach AND your throat are both crying out. Once it goes figurative, picture someone so eager to learn they are 'starving and parched' for books — that hunger/thirst image is exactly the metaphor Chinese uses too.

**例句 / example:** 他很愛學習，對新的東西總是飢渴。
## 供養 — ㄍㄨㄥˋ ㄧㄤˋ · TOCFL-7
**意思 / meaning:** to supply; to provide for one's elders; to support one's parents

**拆解 / breakdown:** 供 (ㄍㄨㄥˋ) means to supply / provide / offer up — the 人 (person) radical on the left shows a person handing something over. 養 (ㄧㄤˋ) means to raise / nourish / support and you already know it (as in raising a child or keeping an animal); note its lower part 食 (food), so 養 is fundamentally about feeding and sustaining. 供 (provide) + 養 (nourish) = to provide for and sustain someone — classically your parents/elders, or to make offerings to gods and ancestors. It is the upward-flowing, dutiful counterpart to ordinary 養.

**記憶 / memory hook:** You already know 養 (to raise/nourish). 供養 is 養 pointed UPWARD: instead of a parent feeding a child, the grown child now supplies (供) and nourishes (養) the parent. Tie it to 面子 culture — 供養 your 父母 is a deep filial duty, the respectful version of 'taking care of' family.

**例句 / example:** 他長大以後，開始供養父母。
## 陌生人 — ㄇㄛˋ ㄕㄥ ㄖㄣˊ · B2
**意思 / meaning:** stranger

**拆解 / breakdown:** 陌生 (ㄇㄛˋ ㄕㄥ) = unfamiliar / strange, plus 人 (ㄖㄣˊ, person) which you know well = an unfamiliar person = a stranger. Inside 陌生: 陌 originally meant a path between fields (its left side is the 阜 mound/earth radical, same wall-like radical as in 防), giving a sense of 'out there on some far road'; 生 here means raw / unfamiliar / not yet known (the same 生 as in 'raw' or 'unripe'). So 陌生 = 'paths you have never walked' = unfamiliar, and 陌生人 caps it with 人.

**記憶 / memory hook:** 陌生 is the exact opposite of a word you may meet, 熟 (familiar/cooked). 生 = raw/unfamiliar, so a 陌生人 is a 'raw person' — someone you have not yet 'cooked' into a friend. Just remember any 人 you do not know is 陌生 (raw); once you 聊天 enough, they stop being 陌生.

**例句 / example:** 以前他是陌生人，後來變成我的好朋友。
## 稱呼 — ㄔㄥ ㄏㄨ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** to call; to address as; form of address

**拆解 / breakdown:** 稱 (ㄔㄥ) means to call / to name / to state (it also means 'to weigh', from naming a quantity). 呼 (ㄏㄨ) means to call out / shout / breathe out — note the 口 (mouth) radical on its left, since calling is done with the mouth (same 口 in many 'speaking' words). 稱 (name) + 呼 (call out with the mouth) = the way you address or refer to someone, e.g. by name, title, or relationship term. It works as both a verb ('to address as') and a noun ('a form of address').

**記憶 / memory hook:** Spot the 口 (mouth) inside 呼 — addressing someone is literally a mouth action. Tie it to 文化 and 面子: choosing the right 稱呼 (老師? name? 長輩 title?) is a politeness minefield, so 稱呼 is the word for 'what do I CALL this person without losing face'.

**例句 / example:** 在這裡，我們稱呼他老師。
## 辯論 — ㄅㄧㄢˋ ㄌㄨㄣˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** debate; argument; to argue over

**拆解 / breakdown:** 辯 (ㄅㄧㄢˋ) means to argue / dispute / defend a position with words — look at its middle: the 言 (speech) radical is sandwiched between two 辛 elements, picturing words wedged between two opposing sides. 論 (ㄌㄨㄣˋ) means to discuss / to reason / a treatise, and it also has the 言 (speech) radical on the left. So 辯論 is doubly about speech: 辯 (argue a side) + 論 (discuss/reason) = a formal debate or structured argument, more orderly than a casual 討論 (discussion, which you know).

**記憶 / memory hook:** Both halves hide 言 (speech) — 辯論 is speech versus speech. Contrast it with 討論 (discuss): in a 討論 friends explore together; in a 辯論 two sides each defend a position and try to win. The doubled 辛 (a 'hardship/sharp' element) flanking 言 in 辯 looks like two debaters glaring across a podium.

**例句 / example:** 他們對這個問題有不同的意見，所以開始辯論。
## 破裂 — ㄆㄛˋ ㄌㄧㄝˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** to burst; to rupture; (of a relationship etc) to break down

**拆解 / breakdown:** 兩個字都帶「破壞」的意思。破 = 破掉、破壞 (你已經學過「破」這個概念，像東西破了)。裂 = 裂開、裂縫 (一條線把東西分成兩半)。合起來:從「破」到「裂」,東西不只是破了,還整個裂開、分開,所以引申到關係「破裂」= 兩個人徹底分開、回不去了。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 想像一個氣球:先「破」(啵一聲),再「裂」成碎片。具體的東西(水管、氣球)會破裂,抽象的東西(友誼、感情、談判)也會破裂。這是 TOCFL-6 的高級書面詞,口語比較少用,看到它通常代表「徹底壞掉、無法修復」,語氣很重。注意:朋友吵架還沒到破裂,破裂是最後決裂的那一步。

**例句 / example:** 因為金錢問題,他們的友誼破裂了。
## 仇恨 — ㄔㄡˊ ㄏㄣˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** to hate; hatred; enmity

**拆解 / breakdown:** 仇 = 仇人、敵人 (有深仇的對象)。恨 = 恨、怨恨 (你可能在「討厭」之後會遇到的更強的字)。兩個近義字疊在一起,把「恨」的程度推到最高:不是普通的討厭,而是深到像對仇人一樣的恨意。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 程度階梯記法:不喜歡 → 討厭 → 恨 → 仇恨。「仇恨」是最頂端、最深最久的那種,常跟戰爭、民族、血海深仇連在一起,不會用在「我仇恨數學課」這種小事上。可當名詞(充滿仇恨)也可當動詞(仇恨某人)。和「破裂」一樣是很重的 TOCFL-6 書面語。

**例句 / example:** 因為錢的問題，他對以前的朋友充滿了仇恨。
## 矛盾 — ㄇㄠˊ ㄉㄨㄣˋ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** contradiction; conflicting views; contradictory

**拆解 / breakdown:** 這是一個成語故事縮成的詞,字面是兩種兵器。矛 = 長槍(攻擊用,刺人的)。盾 = 盾牌(防守用,擋攻擊的)。古代有人同時賣矛和盾,吹噓「我的矛什麼盾都刺得穿」又說「我的盾什麼矛都擋得住」——那拿你的矛刺你的盾會怎樣?自相矛盾。所以「矛盾」= 前後對不上、互相打架的說法或想法。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 記住那個賣武器的人:矛(攻)碰上盾(守),兩邊不可能同時成立 = 矛盾。可指邏輯上的「矛盾」(他說的話很矛盾),也可指人際間的「衝突、不合」(兩人之間有矛盾)。是日常很常用的 TOCFL-5 詞,比「破裂」「仇恨」溫和、好用。

**例句 / example:** 這部電影裡面有很多矛盾的地方。
## 諷刺 — ㄈㄥˋ ㄘˋ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** to satirize; to mock; irony

**拆解 / breakdown:** 諷 = 言字旁(訁/言),代表跟「說話」有關;本義是用拐彎的話來勸或譏笑。刺 = 刺、扎(像針刺人那樣)。合起來:用話像針一樣去「刺」人 = 表面說一套、實際在嘲笑或批評。言旁 + 刺,正好是「用言語去刺」的畫面。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 記成「說話像扎針」:諷刺不是直接罵,而是用反話、酸話讓人不舒服。中文裡常見「很諷刺的是…」(指事情結果剛好相反,很 ironic),也常見「他在諷刺我」(他在酸我)。和「面子」文化很相關——華人不直接撕破臉,常用諷刺繞著講。

**例句 / example:** 他常用話來諷刺社會的現象。
## 幼稚 — ㄧㄡˋ ㄓˋ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** young; childish; puerile

**拆解 / breakdown:** 幼 = 幼小、年幼(你應該見過「幼兒園/幼稚園」的幼)。稚 = 稚嫩、不成熟(禾字旁,本指像幼苗一樣嫩)。兩個都指「小、嫩、未長成」,合起來形容「像小孩一樣不成熟」。臺灣的「幼稚園」就是這個幼稚,本義中性=幼童。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 小心一詞兩義:講小孩本身=年幼(中性);但拿來說大人的想法、行為=罵人「幼稚、不成熟」(貶義)。說「他很幼稚」幾乎都是批評。和「成熟」是相反詞;吵架時說對方幼稚,很傷面子,要小心使用。

**例句 / example:** 他的想法還很幼稚,像小孩一樣。
## 單純 — ㄉㄢ ㄔㄨㄣˊ · TOCFL-4
**意思 / meaning:** simple; pure; unsophisticated

**拆解 / breakdown:** 單 = 單一、單獨(你學過「單」=只有一個,如單身、單字)。純 = 純粹、純的(絞絲旁,本指沒摻雜質的絲,引申為乾淨、不混)。合起來:只有一種、沒有摻雜 = 單純、純粹。也可當副詞「單純地、只是」。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 兩面都是好意:形容人=心思乾淨、好騙、不複雜(個性單純);形容事=不複雜、好處理(問題很單純)。當副詞時=「只是、純粹」(我單純想幫你)。和「複雜」相反。注意它通常是正面或中性,跟貶義的「幼稚」不一樣——單純是心地好,幼稚是不成熟,別搞混。

**例句 / example:** 他的個性很單純,所以很容易相信別人。
## 換取 — ㄏㄨㄢˋ ㄑㄩˇ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** to obtain (sth) in exchange; to exchange (sth) for (sth else)

**拆解 / breakdown:** 兩個你大概已經會的字組成：換 (huàn) = 交換、換掉，你在『換錢』『換衣服』裡見過；取 (qǔ) = 拿、取得 (像『取錢』『取得』)。合起來字面是『用交換的方式去拿到』——也就是『付出某樣東西，換來另一樣東西』。重點：它是動詞，後面接你想得到的東西 (用 A 換取 B = 拿 A 去換到 B)。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 把它想成一筆交易：『換』先丟出去，『取』再收回來。順序就是現實裡的買賣——先給 (換)，後拿 (取)。常考的陷阱是助詞搭配:『用…換取…』這個句型，前面放你付出的，後面放你得到的，別放反了。和情節呼應：人有時想『用錢換取友誼』,但真正的感情換不來。

**例句 / example:** 他用很多錢換取友誼，可是真心的朋友不是這樣來的。
## 委婉 — ㄨㄟˇ ㄨㄢˇ · TOCFL-7
**意思 / meaning:** tactful; euphemistic; (of voice etc) suave

**拆解 / breakdown:** 委 (wěi) 本義有『曲折、彎曲、託付』的味道 (你可能在『委員』『委屈』見過);婉 (wǎn) 本身就是『柔和、含蓄、不直接』。兩字都帶『彎一彎、不走直線』的感覺，疊起來就是『把話繞個彎、說得柔和而不刺人』。通常用來形容說話或拒絕的方式 (委婉地說、委婉地拒絕),也可形容聲音柔美。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 記成『話不直走，拐個彎』——委婉的反義就是『直接』。畫面:一個人不好意思當面說『不』,於是把話彎彎繞繞地講出來，這就是委婉。和情節呼應：在重面子的文化裡，人常用委婉的方式拒絕，好保住對方的臉,而不傷感情。注意它常和『地』連用當副詞:委婉地……。

**例句 / example:** 他不想讓朋友沒面子，所以委婉地拒絕了我。
## 困境 — ㄎㄨㄣˋ ㄐㄧㄥˋ · TOCFL-5
**意思 / meaning:** predicament; plight

**拆解 / breakdown:** 困 (kùn) = 被困住、走不出去 (你大概知道『困難』的困);境 (jìng) = 處境、地方、狀態 (像『環境』的境)。合起來就是『被困住的處境』——一個讓你動彈不得、很難脫身的局面。它是名詞，常和動詞搭配:遇到困境、走出困境、面對困境。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 拆字記:困 = 一個『木』被四面圍住 (口),像被牆關起來;境 = 你所在的『處境』。所以困境就是『四面是牆的處境』。對比:困難 (kùnnán) 偏向『某件事很難』,困境 (kùnjìng) 偏向『整個情況很糟、出不去』。和情節呼應:遇到困境時,真正的朋友會幫你走出來，這比金錢更重要。

**例句 / example:** 我遇到困境的時候，是好朋友幫助我走出來的。
## 高尚 — ㄍㄠ ㄕㄤˋ · TOCFL-6
**意思 / meaning:** noble; lofty; refined

**拆解 / breakdown:** 高 (gāo) = 高 (你早就會的字);尚 (shàng) 有『崇尚、推崇、高貴』的意味 (像『時尚』『崇尚』)。合起來『高 + 尚』= 品格、情操站得很高，值得人尊敬——形容人的道德、行為或趣味很高貴、不庸俗。它是形容詞,常說:高尚的人、高尚的行為、人格高尚。

**記憶 / memory hook:** 把『高』當字面的高度,把整個詞想成『站在道德的高處』。注意它形容的是『品格/精神層面的高』,不是身高,也不是價格高。容易混的是:高尚 (人品高貴) ≠ 高級 (東西檔次高)。和情節呼應:幫助別人而不求回報,這種行為很高尚,值得學習。

**例句 / example:** 他幫助別人不為了錢，這種行為非常高尚，值得我們學習。
